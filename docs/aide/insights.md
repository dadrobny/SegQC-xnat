- [x] knowledge — **project-wide normalisation policy, settled 2026-08-12.** Two rules govern any scaling of a feature or metric. **(1) A normalisation factor must never introduce a supervision dependency.** Anything derived from ground truth — a GT label count, "the levels this scan *should* have", a reference annotation — is supervision, not a feature; scaling by it yields a number that cannot be computed on real segmenter output, which is the setting FACET exists to analyse, and mixes supervision into the feature space.
